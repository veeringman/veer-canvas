3.  PROCEEDINGS

    3.1  Path / link-road civil suit — update

         The Committee discussed the hearing held on 9 August 2026 in the
         pending civil suit concerning the right of path / link road through
         Himuda Housing Colony Sanyard (Society Court Case No. 01; Civil Suit
         ≈ 086/2023, Court of the Senior Civil Judge, Mandi).

         It was assessed that the matter presently appears to stand in favour
         of the colony / Society side. Further conduct of the case is with
         Advocate Shri Shailesh Sharma, appointed counsel.

    3.2  Professional fee of the newly appointed counsel

         The Committee considered the professional fee payable to Advocate
         Shri Shailesh Sharma, newly appointed counsel for the said case.

         It was decided and accepted that a lump-sum professional fee of
         Rs. 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand only) shall be paid to him for
         this case, to be borne equally:

             •  50% (Rs. 25,000/-) by the Society; and
             •  50% (Rs. 25,000/-) by Shri B.C. Sharma, who is also a party
                in the case.

         Shri B.C. Sharma accepted this proposal in the meeting.

         It was confirmed that Rs. 1,500/- has already been paid to the
         counsel by Shri B.C. Sharma toward his share. The pending amount
         shall be paid in due course.

         This decision was put to the Committee and carried.

             For: 4    Against: 0    Abstain: 0    Passed: Yes

    3.3  Unpaid expenses, arrears, and members’ dues

         The Committee discussed the need to read out, and follow up with,
         colony residents whose Society dues are pending.

    3.4  Main gate — painting; street name / number plates

         Painting of the main gate, and installation of name / number plates
         at various street openings and turns (about six to seven places),
         were discussed.

         An amount of about Rs. 15,000/- (Rupees Fifteen Thousand only) was
         approved to be spent on this work.

         (The agenda also listed a guard trial; no separate decision on a
         guard trial was recorded.)

    3.5  Repair of potholes

         Repair of potholes in the colony streets was discussed and deferred,
         as official road-repair work is expected to commence shortly.

    3.6  North-east boundary — fencing

         Fencing of the north-east boundary of the colony was discussed. It
         was decided that this edge shall be secured with appropriate barbed
         wire / fencing, and that about Rs. 10,000/- to Rs. 15,000/- may be
         spent on this from Society funds.

    3.7  Street-cleaning staff

         The need to speak with the street-cleaning staff was also discussed.


4.  RESOLUTION

    RESOLVED THAT a lump-sum professional fee of Rs. 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty
    Thousand only) be paid to Advocate Shri Shailesh Sharma, newly appointed
    counsel for the pending path / link-road civil suit; that 50% of the said
    amount (Rs. 25,000/-) be borne by the Society and 50% (Rs. 25,000/-) by
    Shri B.C. Sharma, who is also a party in the case and who has accepted
    this arrangement; and that Rs. 1,500/- already paid to the counsel by
    Shri B.C. Sharma be treated as a payment toward his share, the balance
    being payable in due course.

        For: 4    Against: 0    Abstain: 0    Carried.
