Certified true copy of resolution

Passed by the Executive Committee of Mandi Housing Welfare Society at its meeting held on 15/08/2026 at Housing Colony Sanyard, Mandi (H.P.), with the sharing of professional fee as recorded in the minutes of the Executive Committee meeting held on 12/09/2026 (Register No. 1/2026).

Subject: Appointment of Advocate Mr. Shailesh Sharma for conduct of pending civil litigation; engagement of Mr. Bimal Sharma for continued guidance; sharing of professional fee between the Society and Shri B.C. Sharma.

The Committee considered that the civil suit concerning the right of path / link road through Himuda Housing Colony Sanyard (pending before the Senior Civil Judge, Mandi, Civil Suit No. ≈ 086/2023, or as numbered on the court record) is of vital importance to the Society and its members; that the case requires active legal representation for further hearings, filings, and proceedings; that Mr. Bimal Sharma, Advocate, has hitherto represented the Society in the matter; and that Mr. Shailesh Sharma, Advocate, has been confirmed by the Committee to take forward the conduct of the case.

RESOLVED THAT Mr. Shailesh Sharma, Advocate, be and is hereby appointed and confirmed as the counsel of the Mandi Housing Welfare Society to take over, conduct, and pursue the aforesaid pending civil suit and all connected applications, hearings, and proceedings before the Hon’ble Court and any other forum as may be required in connection therewith.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT for the conduct of the said case, Mr. Shailesh Sharma, Advocate, shall be paid a lump-sum professional fee of Rs. 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand only) for this case.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT as decided and accepted at the Executive Committee meeting held on 12/09/2026 (Register No. 1/2026; resolution passed For: 4, Against: 0, Abstain: 0), the said professional fee of Rs. 50,000/- shall be borne equally: 50% (Rs. 25,000/-) by the Society from the Society’s funds (Bank of Baroda, Mandi Branch, Account No. 09640100004511, IFSC BARB0MANDIX), in accordance with the Society’s rules and banking instructions; and 50% (Rs. 25,000/-) by Shri B.C. Sharma, who is also a party in the case. Shri B.C. Sharma has accepted this proposal. It is recorded that Rs. 1,500/- has already been paid to the counsel by Shri B.C. Sharma toward his share; the pending amount shall be paid in due course. Court fees, stamps, process, certified copies and similar out-of-pocket expenses of the case, if any, shall be borne separately against bills and are not included in the said professional fee of Rs. 50,000/-.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the conduct of the case shall transfer from Mr. Bimal Sharma, Advocate, to Mr. Shailesh Sharma, Advocate, for all future court work and formal representation; and that Mr. Bimal Sharma, Advocate, having agreed thereto, shall continue to provide guidance and advice to the Society in the same matter as and when required, on terms to be mutually agreed and recorded separately if necessary.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the President and the General Secretary of the Society be and are hereby jointly and severally authorised to: sign the vakalatnama / power to act, engagement letter, and all pleadings, affidavits, applications, and correspondence; brief and instruct Mr. Shailesh Sharma, Advocate, and Mr. Bimal Sharma, Advocate, as appropriate; release payment of the Society’s share of Rs. 25,000/- and any incidental lawful expenses relating to the case; do all other acts necessary to give effect to this resolution.

Certified that the above is a true extract of the resolution passed by the Executive Committee, that it is in force, and that it has not been modified or rescinded.
