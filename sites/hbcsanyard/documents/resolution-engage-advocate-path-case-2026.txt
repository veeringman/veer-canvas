                    MANDI HOUSING WELFARE SOCIETY
              Himuda Housing Colony Sanyard, Mandi (H.P.)
                   Registration No. 467 dated 21/07/2012

                    CERTIFIED TRUE COPY OF RESOLUTION

Certified true copy of the resolution passed by the Executive Committee of
Mandi Housing Welfare Society at its meeting held on ______________ at Housing
Colony Sanyard, Mandi, Himachal Pradesh.

The Committee noted that a civil suit is pending before the learned Senior
Civil Judge, Mandi (Civil Suit No. ≈ 086/2023, number as on the papers on
file), in which the Society / colony association is a defendant, concerning the
alleged right of path / link road through Himuda Housing Colony Sanyardh
(especially near Plot 12-A and Khasra 676/68), including gate / boundary works
and the effect of the SDM order dated 29.11.2022. Pleadings are on record;
further hearings remain. The matter is the Society’s Court Case No. 01.

The Committee further noted that the Society is a body corporate entitled to
sue and be sued and to defend legal proceedings touching its property, rights
and claims (Himachal Pradesh Societies Registration Act, 2006; Rules &
Bye-laws — the Executive Committee conducts all affairs of the Society).
Amounts exceeding the Secretary’s incidental limit are to be paid from the
Society’s bank account, operated by any two among the President, Vice-President,
Treasurer and Secretary.

The Committee considered the need to engage fresh counsel to take the pending
path case forward, and the professional fee agreed with Advocate Shailesh Sharma
of ₹50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand only) for the case.

It was therefore unanimously:

RESOLVED THAT Advocate Shailesh Sharma (enrollment / Bar details to be filled
on the vakalatnama: ________________________) be and is hereby engaged as
advocate for Mandi Housing Welfare Society to appear, plead, act and take all
necessary steps on behalf of the Society in the said pending civil suit
relating to the path / link road through Himuda Housing Colony Sanyardh (Civil
Suit ≈ 086/2023 and any connected application in the same court), in
supersession of any earlier engagement of counsel for this matter to the extent
a fresh vakalatnama is required.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the professional fee of the said advocate for conducting
the case be and is hereby sanctioned at ₹50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand only),
to be paid from the Society’s account with Bank of Baroda, Mandi Branch
(Account No. 09640100004511, IFSC BARB0MANDIX), by cheque or electronic transfer
signed / authorised jointly by any two of the authorised signatories, in one or
more instalments as the Treasurer may record against this resolution. Court
fees, stamps, process, certified copies, typing and similar out-of-pocket
expenses of the litigation shall be paid by the Society separately against bills
and are not included in the said professional fee.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the President and the General Secretary, jointly or
either of them, be and are hereby authorised to execute the vakalatnama and all
pleadings, applications, affidavits, replies, compromises (if any, only with a
further decision of the Committee), and other papers required to give effect to
this engagement; to instruct the advocate; to receive notices; and to do all
acts necessary for the conduct of the case.

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Treasurer be and is hereby authorised to process
payment of the sanctioned professional fee and of verified out-of-pocket bills,
and that a certified copy of this resolution, signed by the President and the
General Secretary and bearing the Society seal, be placed on the case file and
furnished to the advocate.

Certified that the above is a true extract of the resolution passed at the
meeting on the date stated above, and that it is in force and has not been
modified or rescinded.
