Section 1

Overview

What the dispute is about

Residents of Village Sanyardh (Jol) filed a suit for declaration and permanent / mandatory injunction claiming a long-standing right of path/road through land that became Housing Board Colony Sanyardh (HIMUDA), to reach:

  • their remaining private land (including land abutting Khasra No. 676/68); and
  • the main road.

They allege that after Plot 12-A was purchased by private defendants, the path was obstructed (gate, barbed wire, boundary wall), and that an SDM Sadar Mandi order dated 29.11.2022 wrongly limited village use of the link road to emergencies / consent of defendants.

This is framed as an access / right-of-way case — not a claim to take over colony plots.

Section 3

Suit land

Location

Section 4

Timeline (as alleged in the plaint)

  1. Ancestral period / 60+ years Plaintiffs and forefathers allegedly used the path openly, continuously and peacefully as of right to reach the main road and their land.
  2. 9 September 2003 Mutation no. 676 — land in para 1(b) transferred to HIMUDA for Housing Board Colony Sanyardh.
  3. After acquisition Defendants 1–3 allegedly converted the existing path into a road on colony land; village use continued.
  4. 5 March 2012 NOC / permission to convert existing path into a link road through Housing Board Colony Sanyardh (later allegedly withdrawn unilaterally after ~10 years of use).
  5. 2012 On public representation, State of H.P. constructed a road for Village Sanyardh (Jol) residents, connected with the HIMUDA path/road on land 1(b), touching Khasra 676/68. Peaceful use since 2012 alleged; pleaded as the only available approach.
  6. October 2022 Defendants 4 & 5 (Plot 12-A purchasers), allegedly in connivance with others, began damaging / obstructing the path/road.
  7. 29 November 2022 SDM Sadar Mandi passed a conditional order after spot reports (see next section).
  8. 2 January 2023 Alleged complete blockade by boundary wall, barbed wire and gate on the path abutting Khasra 676/68.
  9. 10 January 2023 Plaint filed at Mandi; supporting affidavit of Biri Singh attached.

Section 5

Plaintiffs’ main claims

  • Right of path/road through suit land (especially para 1(b)) exists from ancestral times and survives HIMUDA acquisition of colony land.
  • After 2012, the constructed link road has been the practical (and pleaded as only) approach to the main road and to land in para 1(c).
  • Plot 12-A owners (defendants 4 & 5) illegally obstructed the path from October 2022, culminating in full blockade on 2.1.2023.
  • HIMUDA / colony (defendants 1–3) allegedly imposed / later withdrew conditions via the 5.3.2012 permission in a manner that debars village path rights.
  • Requests to stop obstruction were ignored; hence the civil suit.

Section 6

SDM order challenged (29.11.2022)

After a complaint by village residents, SDM Sadar Mandi called spot reports from the subordinate revenue agency and, as per the plaint, ordered that:

  • residents may use the link road only in emergency;
  • defendants may construct a gate on the road at the boundary of suit land in para 1(b) abutting Khasra No. 676/68; and
  • plaintiffs may use the road only with the consent of the defendants.

The plaintiffs say this wrongly and illegally debars even pedestrian use, and seek a declaration that the order is null and void and has no effect on their right of path.

Section 7

Reliefs prayed

  1. Declaration that the plaintiffs have a right of path/road through the suit land detailed in para 1(b) to approach the land in para 1(c) and the main road.
  2. Declaration that the SDM Sadar Mandi order dated 29.11.2022 is null and void and does not affect that right.
  3. Mandatory injunction directing defendants to remove obstructions (gate, barbed wire, etc.) from the path/road.
  4. Permanent prohibitory injunction restraining defendants from interfering with the path/road over the suit land.
  5. Costs and any other relief the court finds just.
