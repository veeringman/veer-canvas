RESOLUTION: CHANGE OF AUTHORISED SIGNATORIES — BANK OF BARODA, MANDI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Committee noted that the Society maintains Savings / Current Account
No. 09640100004511 with Bank of Baroda, Mandi Branch (IFSC BARB0MANDIX),
in the name of Mandi Housing Welfare Society, and that a new Executive
Committee has assumed office.

The Committee further noted Rule 10 of the Society’s Rules & Bye-laws:
amounts of the Society shall be kept in a bank account in the Society’s
name, operated by any two among the President, Vice-President, Treasurer
and Secretary.

It was therefore unanimously:

RESOLVED THAT, in supersession of all earlier resolutions, mandates and
authorised signatories in respect of Account No. 09640100004511 with
Bank of Baroda, Mandi Branch (IFSC BARB0MANDIX), and any other account
of the Society with the said Bank, the following four office bearers of
Mandi Housing Welfare Society be and are hereby authorised to operate
the said account(s):

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the said account shall be operated jointly by
ANY TWO of the above four authorised signatories, and that the Bank of
