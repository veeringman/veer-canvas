Around town

Places

A first map of Mandi and nearby days out — not an official tourism portal.

Temple

Bhutnath Temple

Shiva shrine at the heart of Mandi town — a landmark for the bazaar and the river quarter.

Confluence

Panchvaktra Temple

At the meeting of the Beas and Suketi — one of the city’s oldest sacred sites.

River

Victoria Bridge

The Beas crossing that still orients the town — walk, photograph, and watch the water change with the season.

Market

Indira Market

Daily bazaar for produce, cloth, and errands. Hours and stalls shift — treat this as a pointer, not a listing.

Nearby

Rewalsar

Lakeside town of gompas and pilgrimage, a short ride from Mandi.

Day trip

Prashar Lake

High lake and wooden pagoda temple above the valley — weather and road conditions decide the day.
