Around town

Places

A first map of Mandi and nearby days out — not an official tourism portal.

Bhutnath Temple

Shiva shrine at the heart of Mandi town — a landmark for the bazaar and the river quarter.

Confluence

Panchvaktra Temple

At the meeting of the Beas and Suketi — one of the city’s oldest sacred sites.

River

Victoria Bridge

The Beas crossing that still orients the town — walk, photograph, and watch the water change with the season.

Market

Indira Market

Daily bazaar for produce, cloth, and errands. Hours and stalls shift — treat this as a pointer, not a listing.

Nearby

Rewalsar

Lakeside town of gompas and pilgrimage, a short ride from Mandi.

Day trip

Prashar Lake

High lake and wooden pagoda temple above the valley — weather and road conditions decide the day.

About this hub

A civic website for Mandi. Not an official government property.

Not the Municipal Corporation

City of Mandi is an independent civic hub — news, places, services, and neighbourhoods. It is not a website of the Municipal Corporation Mandi, the District Administration, HIMUDA, or any government department. Nothing here is an official notice, tender, tax receipt, or licence.

Same civic craft as the colony home

The layout follows the public homepage built for Housing Colony Sanyard, with a Mandi palette — teal-slate, copper, and river mist — so the city hub and the colony can sit side by side without looking like one office.
