> **Listen. Understand. Remember. Predict.**

**WiseEars** is an AI-native Conversation Intelligence Platform that transforms conversations into structured, searchable, and actionable knowledge.

It goes far beyond recording and transcription. WiseEars understands context, tracks decisions, extracts commitments, connects discussions across time, and builds a living knowledge base from every conversation.

Whether it's a board meeting, project review, client call, lecture, interview, legal consultation, healthcare discussion, or personal brainstorming session, WiseEars becomes your trusted memory—helping you recall the past, understand the present, and prepare for what's next.

---

## Vision

Every meaningful conversation contains knowledge.

Unfortunately, most of that knowledge is forgotten, scattered across recordings, notes, emails, chat messages, and documents.

WiseEars changes that.

Instead of treating conversations as isolated recordings, WiseEars continuously transforms them into connected knowledge. Every meeting, call, lecture, and discussion contributes to an evolving understanding of people, projects, decisions, tasks, and ideas.

The result is a searchable, intelligent memory that grows richer over time.

---

## Core Principles

* **Capture** — Record conversations with high-quality audio today and video in future releases.
* **Understand** — Identify speakers, topics, decisions, questions, risks, commitments, and action items.
* **Remember** — Build a persistent, searchable knowledge graph across conversations.
* **Connect** — Link people, projects, documents, and discussions across weeks, months, and years.
* **Predict** — Surface unresolved items, upcoming risks, and likely next actions.
* **Protect** — Privacy-first architecture with encryption, user control, and enterprise deployment options.

### AI Memory

Search naturally using questions such as:

* "What did we decide about authentication?"
* "Show every discussion involving Kubernetes."
* "When was the budget approved?"
* "Who committed to completing this task?"

WiseEars answers using the complete history of conversations instead of individual transcripts.

### FutureWatch

WiseEars continuously analyzes historical discussions to identify:

* Pending commitments
* Missing approvals
* Unresolved risks
* Recurring issues
* Likely follow-up meetings
* Recommended next actions

### Studio

Generate professional content from conversations.

* Executive briefings
* Meeting minutes
* Podcasts
* Voice summaries
* Shareable reports
