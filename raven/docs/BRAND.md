# RAVEN brand

RAVEN is a raven in profile: a dark head, an amber beak aimed to the right, one light eye. The beak is the mark of intent becoming action. Violet sits on the crown. Teal sits on the throat. Those two colors are the iridescence of the plumage, used as thin light, not as a fill.

## Name and line

- Name: **RAVEN**
- Line: **From intent to action**
- Descriptor: The runtime for agentic applications

Set the name in capitals with open letter-spacing. The line is smaller, tracked wider, and quieter.

## Color

| Mist | `#F4F6FB` | Wordmark on night |
| Silver | `#9AA3B8` | Tagline on night |
| Slate | `#5E6578` | Tagline on light |
| Violet | `#7A6CFF` | Crown light |
| Teal | `#2EE6C7` | Throat light |
| Amber | `#F5B942` | Beak, eye glint, the single action accent |

Amber is the only accent that should appear in product UI. Violet and teal stay on the mark.

Do not redraw the beak in another color. Do not add a second bird. Do not set the wordmark in a different face for the logo; product UI may use Outfit, which is the face the outlines came from.

Clear space around the lockup is at least the height of the eye.

## Icons

Stroke icons on a 24 grid, 1.6px stroke, round caps.

Perceive, Reason, Plan, Act, Verify, Adapt are the loop.

Intent, Policy, Evidence, Capability, Memory, and Goal are the primitives.

## Voice

Short sentences. Name the outcome, the constraint, and what was verified. Do not call RAVEN a chatbot, an assistant, or a copilot.
